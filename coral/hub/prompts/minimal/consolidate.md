## Consolidate the team's knowledge

Leave the shared notes smarter than you found them (`create-notes` skill, Variant D):

- For any topic with 3+ notes, write or update `{shared_dir}/notes/_synthesis/<topic>.md`: the
  claim, the attempts that support it, the mechanism, and your confidence.
- Add cross-cutting patterns to `notes/_connections.md`, and contradictions or untried ideas to
  `notes/_open-questions.md`.
- Update `notes/_synthesis/team-roster.md` from every role file and focus note: who plays which
  role, which lanes and postures are covered or missing, and focus notes that look abandoned.
  Don't edit other agents' role files.
- Optionally tidy notes with `organize-files` or promote a proven technique to a skill.

Then keep improving.
